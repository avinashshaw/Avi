Product Reviews — Import / Export

Import reviews from a CSV file or export your existing reviews back to CSV. Reviews are saved to your store as metaobjects.

Import reviews (CSV)

Required CSV columns

Your header row must include these columns (extra columns are ignored):

rating,handle,author,email,title,content,images,created_at,country_code
  • rating — integer 1–5 (required)
  • handle — product handle the review belongs to (required)
  • content — the review body text (required)
  • author, email, title, created_at, country_code — optional
  • images — optional, comma-separated image URLs inside one quoted cell
